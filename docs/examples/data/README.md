# Example data

Observations used by the gallery scripts, saved here so the examples keep
working after the online services stop serving them. All are public.

| File | What it is | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `metars_2026102706.csv` | Temperature, dewpoint and sea-level pressure from 1,965 METAR reports across the US, southern Canada and northern Mexico at 06 UTC 27 October 2026 | [aviationweather.gov data API](https://aviationweather.gov/data/api/) |
| `raob_KBMX_2011042718.csv` | The 18 UTC special radiosonde from Birmingham, Alabama (KBMX) on 27 April 2011, during the Super Outbreak | [Iowa Environmental Mesonet](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/archive/raob/) |
| `asos_BUF_blizzard.csv` | Routine and special observations from Buffalo, New York (KBUF), 22 to 27 December 2022, through the blizzard | [Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS archive](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/download.phtml) |
| `spc_tornadoes_20211210.csv` | The 71 tornadoes of 10 and 11 December 2021 from the Storm Prediction Center's database (times in CST, path lengths in miles) | [SPC severe weather database](https://www.spc.noaa.gov/wcm/) |
| `asos_AMA_2025.csv` | Hourly wind speed and direction at Amarillo, Texas (KAMA) for 2025 | Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS archive |

Units are as the sources give them: METAR temperatures in °C, ASOS
temperatures in °F, wind speeds in knots, pressures in hPa (mb) and
precipitation in inches.
